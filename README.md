### 🎓 Background & Journey

**From iOS to AI:**  
Started coding at 8 on a Commodore 64. Built 100+ mobile/web apps over 15 years. Now applying that product thinking and engineering discipline to solve real problems in healthcare AI.

**Why Healthcare + Arabic?**  
Most healthcare AI tools fail in the Middle East because they're English-first. We're fixing that. Arabic is complex (28 dialects, right-to-left, diacritics), medical terminology doesn't translate cleanly, and EMR systems vary widely. These constraints make the problem interesting - and the impact meaningful.

**Philosophy:**  
Great CTOs write code. Technology serves people. The best products solve real problems for underserved markets.
